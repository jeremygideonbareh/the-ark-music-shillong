Chapter I — The ARK

A small room in the hills, built to carry music.

Shillong has always been a city that sings. On the third of December, 2022, The ARK opened its doors to give that song a proper home — a place where a child, a teenager or an adult can learn music the right way: theory and technique, patience and practice, and a stage to grow toward.

Founded by

Mr. Ramesh Kumar

  • 15years teaching music
  • Gr. 8Western Classical Vocal Performance — Trinity College of Music, London

“The final aim and purpose of all music is none other than the glorification of God and the refreshment of the soul.”

J. S. Bach — the line that circles The ARK’s seal

Chapter III — One-year foundation course

Forty-eight lessons. One year. A foundation for life.

A complete beginner’s programme that builds musicianship through theory, technique and practical learning — four one-hour sessions a month, for twelve months.
